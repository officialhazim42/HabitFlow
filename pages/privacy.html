Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 13, 2026 • Version 1.2

1. Introduction & Mission

At HabitFlow, crafted by independent software creator Hazim Bashir (حازم بشیر), your digital privacy is our foremost priority. We believe habit tracking and self-improvement tools should empower users without compromising their personal information. This Privacy Policy transparently outlines how data is handled across HabitFlow.

2. Data Collection & Processing

We collect only the minimum data required to deliver core functionality:

  • Account Credentials: When you register using email and password, authentication is processed securely by Supabase. Passwords are never stored in plaintext and undergo salted one-way cryptographic hashing (bcrypt/argon2).
  • Profile Data: Display name and optional avatar URLs are stored in our protected profiles table with Row Level Security (RLS).
  • Habit & Progress Data: Your customized habits, routine categories, and completion dates are stored in user-scoped tables and are protected by strict Row Level Security policies. No user can access or query another user's habits.
  • Social Sign-in (OAuth): If you authenticate via Google or Facebook OAuth, we receive only your verified email and primary name provided by the provider's token.

3. Client-Side Storage & Cookies

HabitFlow uses local storage exclusively for persisting your authentication session tokens and client preferences (such as selected theme). We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking scripts.

4. Row Level Security & Infrastructure

Our database architecture relies on Supabase PostgreSQL with mandatory Row Level Security policies enabled across all tables. Queries require valid JWT authentication tokens issued directly to the user. Client-side requests are strictly validated both in the browser interface and at the database boundary.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

You maintain complete ownership of your habit records. You have the right to inspect, export, or permanently delete your account and associated habits at any time through the in-app settings or by contacting the developer.
